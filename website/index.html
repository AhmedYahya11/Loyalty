Points & wallet

An immutable double-entry points ledger with active, pending and reserved balances — accurate to the last point.

⭐

Tiers

Bronze→Platinum tiers that auto-progress on spend, points or visits and multiply how fast members earn.

🎁

Rewards & QR coupons

A reward catalog customers redeem for points — reserve → confirm at the counter with a QR coupon.

🏅

Gamification

Achievements, daily streaks, time-boxed challenges and leaderboards that keep customers coming back.

👥

Referrals

Every member gets a shareable code/QR; both sides are rewarded automatically when a referral completes.

🎟️

Digital stamp cards

"Buy 10, get 1 free" punch cards — conditioned by service/category, award & reset when full.

🎲

Prize draws

Purchases, bookings or app-shares earn weighted entries; pick a fair, weighted-random winner.

🎂

Birthday & campaigns

Scheduled birthday rewards and a full campaign engine with conditions, stacking, limits and budgets.

🧭

RFM personas

📱

Customer app

A mobile loyalty card: balance, tier, badges, coupons and history — opened from a secure link.

API & webhooks

One generic events API, HMAC-signed webhooks, and an integration kit to plug into any POS or app.

Walaa is a complete, multi-tenant loyalty & rewards platform. You send it customer events (purchases, visits, sign-ups…) through one API and it runs the points ledger, tiers, rewards, gamification, referrals, stamp cards, prize draws and analytics — with an admin dashboard and a customer-facing app.
